## Six shops, one list

The Hungarian Competition Authority's price monitor publishes the shelf prices of the big chains every day: Lidl, Aldi, Penny, Spar, Tesco, Auchan, around 3 700 food products. Kalmio now reads it every morning, and at the bottom of the shopping list shows what the same list would cost at each chain, in whole packs.

We do not reprice anything behind your back. Your list still prices from the shop you switched on under Shops. The comparison is a fact, not a decision; the decision is yours.

## What the first week showed

On a real 44-line list, Lidl was 23 percent cheaper than Kifli on the lines both could price, Aldi six percent, Spar four. Auchan and Tesco were dearer. Milk between 249 and 295 forints, chicken breast between 1 721 and 1 950 a kilo, eggs 45 to 50 forints apiece.

There is an honest limit. The price monitor watches a basic basket; feta, basil, tahini and cashews are not in it. Of the 44 lines, the chains could price between 17 and 23. Where we cannot price a line, we do not guess; we mark it as missing.

## What it is good for now

If the comparison says your weekly list is twenty thousand cheaper at Lidl, you can do two things. Switch Lidl on under Shops and your list is built with their prices for a trip to the store. Or keep Kifli for the convenience and know what you pay for it. Both are good answers. The bad answer is not knowing.

## What will not happen

We will not fill a Lidl basket for you. Lidl, Aldi and Penny have no webshop to put anything into. The price-monitor chains are prices in Kalmio, not handoffs, and that is how they stay.
