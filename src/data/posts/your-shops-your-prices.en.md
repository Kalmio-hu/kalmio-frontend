## One list, your shop

Until now Kalmio knew the prices of a single store. If you shop at the Lidl around the corner, the forints next to your list came from someone else's shelf, and the weekly total was a guess dressed as a number.

From now on you decide under Settings › Shops which stores' prices we use. Switch one on, switch one off, and every line of the shopping list shows the cheapest pack among the shops you allowed: name, pack size, price, and how many packs to take off the shelf.

## What changes underneath

A shop in Kalmio is not a logo. It is three things together:

- **a product catalogue** – which product on the shelf matches which ingredient, in what pack;
- **prices** – refreshed daily, sale price and the sale's end date included;
- **handoff** – where the shop allows it, the list goes into its basket with one tap.

Each shop carries this data in its own right, and the planner, the shopping list and the cost of a recipe all read from that one source. There are no two prices for the same chicken breast.

## Why we do not mix shops

It would be easy to price every ingredient wherever it is cheapest. On paper such a list is unbeatable; in reality it means six shops on a Saturday morning. So the list always prices from the shops you switched on, and the comparison is a separate view: it shows what the same list would cost elsewhere without scrambling your basket.

## What we ask of you

Open Shops and switch on the one or two stores where you actually buy. That is all. Your next list arrives with their prices, and the weekly total now says what the till will say.
