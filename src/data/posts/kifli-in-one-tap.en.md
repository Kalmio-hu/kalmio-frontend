## The list that walks into the basket by itself

Kifli.hu is the first shop where Kalmio does more than show prices: it starts the shopping. Link your account and the weekly list lands in your Kifli basket with one tap, every line as the right product in the right number of packs. Payment and delivery stay with Kifli, as before.

## How it works

1. Settings › Shops › Kifli.hu › **Link account**.
2. Sign in at Kifli. Afterwards the browser lands on an address it cannot open. That is expected.
3. Copy the full address from the address bar and paste it into the Kalmio dialog. Done.

Yes, the third step is a detour. Kifli currently allows automatic linking only for apps running on your own machine, and we are a website. We have asked them for the proper route; until it arrives, the copy-and-paste happens once and the link stays.

## What goes into the basket

The list's "still needed" amounts, rounded to whole packs. If you have 250 g of yogurt at home and the plan asks for 300, one tub goes to Kifli, not two. Pantry staples (spices, oil, vinegar) go in too, but the weekly total shows them on a separate line: you do not rebuy those every week.

Whatever Kifli does not carry, Kalmio skips and tells you which lines. We do not guess on your behalf.

## What we do not do

We do not pay in your name and we do not store a card. We do not read your order history. The Kifli access token is stored encrypted, and you can cut the link any time under Shops.
