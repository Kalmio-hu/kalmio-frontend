## Where we are

Autumn was about shopping. Kalmio now knows six chains and two webshops with daily prices; it fills the Kifli basket in one tap; the list says what you pay at the till; the planner works with a daily budget, single-course meals and cost-saver substitutions. A real week started at 65 000 forints and now sits around 25 to 30 000, with the same protein.

This post is about what is still missing.

## What arrives in the coming weeks

**Kifli in one step.** Linking still takes a copy-and-paste, because Kifli only allows automatic sign-in for apps running on your own machine. We have asked for our own client; once it is issued, the third step disappears.

**More hits in the price monitor.** The chains can price half to two thirds of a list today. The missing part has two halves: what the monitor does not watch (we cannot help there) and what we have not yet recognised on the shelf (we can, week by week).

**More substitutions.** Fourteen rules are the start. The chef brings fresh-to-dried herbs, Greek-to-plain yogurt and the dairy pairs next, each with one sentence on what it does to the taste.

**Faster fixes.** What we broke this morning we fixed this afternoon; for that we rewrote the release process so a small fix is live in four minutes.

## What we ask

Use Shops, set a daily budget, and look at the comparison at the bottom of the list. If the numbers do not say what the till says, write to us. That feedback is next week.
